How to use this view

  • Search by process name, mnemonic, or any differential diagnosis.
  • Filter by specialty to reduce the list.
  • Select several processes to compare overlaps.
  • Drag circles to clear space and reveal diagnoses more easily.
  • Use “Auto-arrange circles” to reorganize the layout automatically.
