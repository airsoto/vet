Tool information

This tool evaluates electrocardiographic parameters related to the P wave in dogs, with the aim of providing educational and clinical support for studying the diagnostic reliability of ECG for detecting left atrial enlargement.

Using an ECG image, the application allows scale calibration, manual P-wave measurements, and automatic calculation of several parameters: P-wave duration and voltage, estimated P-wave area, PR interval, PR segment, P/PR ratio, Macruz index, P-wave electrical axis, and heart rate.

Results are compared with reference values and cut-off points derived from the doctoral study, providing an orientative interpretation of the possible relationship between P-wave abnormalities and left atrial enlargement. This tool does not issue a definitive diagnosis; it is intended for educational use and clinical decision support. Final interpretation must be performed by a veterinarian considering the full ECG, clinical history, physical examination, and, when indicated, echocardiography.

What does each measured parameter represent?

Parameter What it measures Clinical/technical meaning Unit
DP — P-wave duration Time from the beginning to the end of the P wave. Represents the duration of atrial depolarization. An increase may be associated with atrial conduction abnormalities or left atrial enlargement. seconds
VP — P-wave voltage Height of the P wave from the isoelectric line to its highest point. Reflects the electrical amplitude of atrial depolarization. It may increase in some atrial abnormalities. mV
AP — P-wave area Estimated P-wave area using the formula DP × VP / 2. Combines duration and voltage into a single parameter. It is a triangular approximation of the electrical magnitude represented by the P wave. mV·s
DPR — PR interval duration Time from the beginning of the P wave to the beginning of the QRS complex. Includes atrial depolarization and atrioventricular conduction time up to the onset of ventricular activation. seconds
PPR — P/PR ratio Ratio between P-wave duration and PR interval duration, expressed as a percentage. Indicates what proportion of the PR interval is occupied by the P wave. It increases when the P wave represents a larger part of the PR interval. %
PR segment Time from the end of the P wave to the beginning of the QRS complex. Represents the interval between the end of atrial activation and the onset of ventricular activation. It is required to calculate the Macruz index. seconds
IMa — Macruz index Ratio between DP and the PR segment. Relates P-wave duration to the PR segment. It may help assess atrial abnormalities when the PR segment is positive and measurable. unitless
EP — P-wave electrical axis Mean direction of atrial depolarization in the frontal plane. It is calculated from P-wave voltage in the frontal plane leads. It provides an estimate of the overall direction of atrial activation. degrees °
FC — Heart rate Number of beats per minute, calculated from the distance between two consecutive R waves. Helps contextualize electrocardiographic parameters and may contribute to the global interpretation of the tracing. bpm

How to read the statistical analysis

Parameter: name of the electrocardiographic value being evaluated.

Z: indicates how many standard deviations above or below the reference mean the patient’s measurement lies.

Percentil aprox.: estimates the percentage of the reference sample that would fall below that value.

Scale: simplified visual representation of the patient’s position relative to the distribution. The central green band indicates the normality region; the dot shows where the current measurement is located.

1. Patient and ECG

Images and measurements are processed locally in your browser. No data are sent to any server.

2. Calibration

Mark a single 5 mm reference on the grid. This scale is used for both time and voltage.

At 50 mm/s, 5 mm = 0.10 s. With a gain of 1 cm = 1 mV, 5 mm = 0.5 mV.

3. Measurement points

Select a button and click on the ECG image to place the corresponding point. You can also use guided measurement to proceed step by step without pressing each button manually.

Guided mode inactive.

4. Leads and clinical data

For the P-wave axis, you can use voltages measured with points or enter them manually. P-wave voltage in lead II usually matches the “P-wave height” measurement if the lead measured in the image is DII. For heart rate, mark two consecutive R waves; if you do not mark them, you may enter heart rate manually.

HR by R-R: at 50 mm/s = 3000 / mm between R waves; at 25 mm/s = 1500 / mm between R waves.

How to interpret these results

Cut-off points used: patients with DP, VP, AP, DPR, PPR, IMa, and FC values above the considered discrimination limit (mean + one standard deviation) show significantly higher echocardiographic left atrial diameter and area indices relative to the aorta.

P-wave–related parameters can support the assessment of atrial abnormalities, especially left atrial enlargement in dogs with chronic mitral valve degeneration. The tool prioritizes specificity and control of false positives; it does not replace complete ECG interpretation or echocardiography.

Sensitivity

Probability that the test is positive when the abnormality is truly present.

Specificity

Probability that the test is negative when the abnormality is not present.

PPV

Probability that the patient has the abnormality when the test is positive.

NPV

Probability that the patient does not have the abnormality when the test is negative.

LR+

How much the probability of disease increases when the result is positive.

LR−

How much the probability of disease decreases when the result is negative.

Methodological help and brief bibliography

Measurement on a scanned or photographed image depends on photo quality, focus, perspective, rotation, compression, and grid sharpness. Use flat images with no tilt and with a visible grid. The single 5 mm reference assumes there is no differential deformation between horizontal and vertical axes. Heart rate is calculated by measuring the distance between two consecutive R waves: HR = 60 × paper speed / R-R distance in mm.
